# Phase 0 — Turn-Taking Scripts

**The 30 filmed scripts that measure turn-taking (test level L4): when Vaani cuts people off, stops for no reason, stops too slowly, or leaves awkward silences. Version 0 is measured on the audio; version 0.5 is measured on the same takes with their video (RE-D13). These takes can never be re-recorded once version 0 is measured, so film them carefully.**

Status: v1 · 19 Oct 2026 · Part of Phase 0 · How to film: `02_recording_guide.md` section 4

---

## 1. How the scripts are played into a call

A test robot joins a real call with Vaani and plays the recordings as if a person were speaking.

- **One-segment scripts** (groups A and C): the robot plays segment 1 and listens for Vaani's reply.
- **Two-segment scripts** (groups B and D): segment 1 is a question that makes Vaani give a long answer. The robot then plays **segment 2 a fixed time after Vaani starts speaking**, shown as "+2.5 s". That way a "haan" or an interruption lands in the middle of Vaani's answer, whatever she happens to say.

So segment 2 always **starts with you quietly listening**, as if Vaani has just begun talking, then the action happens at the stated time.

## 2. What each group measures

| Group | Scripts | Measures (from `V0_07` L4) | A correct Vaani |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Thinking pauses** | TT01–TT10 | **Cut-offs**: Vaani starts talking during a planned pause | Waits through the pause, replies only after the person finishes |
| **B. Small sounds while Vaani talks** | TT11–TT18 | **Wrong stops**: Vaani stops for "hmm", "haan", a laugh, a cough or background sound | Keeps talking |
| **D. Real interruptions** | TT19–TT24 | **Slow stops**: Vaani takes more than 0.5 s to go quiet after a real interruption | Stops within 0.5 s and listens |
| **C. Clear finishes** | TT25–TT30 | **Awkward silences**: nobody speaks for more than 3 s after the person has clearly finished | Replies promptly |

| Filmer | Scripts |

Ideally F1 to F3 include both men and women, and at least one Hinglish-first speaker.

## 4. Rules for every take

1. **2 seconds** of quiet listening at the start of every segment, **3 seconds** at the end.
2. Time pauses with a stopwatch on a phone just below the camera. Within half a second is fine.
3. Say the words as written, but in your natural voice. Small changes are fine; tell the founder so the transcript matches.
4. Do the face actions written in *italics*. Keep them natural, not acted.
5. One segment per file: `tt_F2_TT14_s1.mp4`, `tt_F2_TT14_s2.mp4`.
6. If a take goes wrong, film it again. Keep only the good take.

**Hidden** scripts (9 of 30) are filmed the same way and used only at phase gates and release.

## Group A — Thinking pauses (cut-offs)

**TT01 · English · 2 s pause · working**
Segment 1: "I was thinking of taking a gap year, but..." *eyes drift up, mouth slightly open* **[pause 2 s]** "...my parents won't agree." *Close mouth, look back at the screen.*

**TT02 · English · 4 s pause with "umm" · working**
Segment 1: "The thing I really want is... umm..." *look slightly down and to the side* **[pause 4 s]** "...more time for myself." *Look back at the screen.*

**TT03 · English · 6 s pause, looking away · hidden**
Segment 1: "If I'm honest, the hardest part is..." *look away to one side, small frown* **[pause 6 s]** "...not knowing who to ask." *Look back at the screen.*

**TT04 · Hinglish · 2 s pause with "matlab" · working**
Segment 1: "Mujhe lagta hai ki, matlab..." *eyes up* **[pause 2 s]** "...main thoda ruk jaaun." *Look back at the screen.*

**TT05 · Hinglish · 4 s pause, trailing "so" · working**
Segment 1: "Office mein sab theek hai, so..." *mouth half open, eyes away* **[pause 4 s]** "...bas manager ka thoda issue hai." *Look back at the screen.*

**TT06 · Hindi · 6 s pause · hidden**
Segment 1: "Sach bataun toh..." *look down, slow breath* **[pause 6 s]** "...mujhe khud nahi pata." *Look back at the screen.*

**TT07 · English · two pauses in one turn · working**
Segment 1: "First I'll finish my degree..." **[pause 3 s]** "...then maybe a job..." *small head tilt* **[pause 2 s]** "...or maybe not." *Look back at the screen.*

**TT08 · Hinglish · 4 s pause inside a list · working**
Segment 1: "Mujhe teen cheezein chahiye: time, paisa, aur..." *eyes up, thinking* **[pause 4 s]** "...thodi peace." *Small smile, look back at the screen.*

**TT09 · English · 4 s pause with fan noise · hidden**
Ceiling fan at full speed for the whole segment.
Segment 1: "I wanted to tell you about..." *look away* **[pause 4 s]** "...what happened at college today." *Look back at the screen.*

**TT10 · Hinglish · 5 s pause, looking down at phone · working**
Segment 1: "Kal main gaya tha, aur..." *look down as if checking a phone* **[pause 5 s]** "...wahan koi tha hi nahi." *Look back at the screen.*

## Group B — Small sounds while Vaani talks (wrong stops)

In every script here, segment 1 asks for a long answer; segment 2 starts with you listening.

**TT11 · "hmm" with a nod · working**
Segment 1: "Can you tell me how to plan a weekend trip from Mumbai?" *Look at the screen.*
Segment 2 (play at +2 s): *listen 2 s* → "Hmm." *small nod* → *keep listening 6 s.*

**TT12 · Hinglish · two "haan"s · working**
Segment 1: "Mujhe batao ki exam ki preparation kaise shuru karun?"
Segment 2 (play at +2.5 s): *listen 2 s* → "Haan." *nod* → *listen 3 s* → "Haan haan." *nod* → *keep listening 5 s.*

**TT13 · "accha" with a small smile · hidden**
Segment 1: "Explain why people find it so hard to wake up early."
Segment 2 (play at +3 s): *listen 2 s* → "Accha." *small smile* → *keep listening 6 s.*

**TT14 · short laugh · working**
Segment 1: "Tell me a funny way to explain what an AI is."
Segment 2 (play at +3 s): *listen 2 s* → *short natural laugh, about 1 s* → *keep listening, smiling, 6 s.*

**TT15 · one cough · working**
Segment 1: "What are some easy dinners I can cook in twenty minutes?"
Segment 2 (play at +2 s): *listen 2 s* → *one cough, head turned slightly away* → *look back, keep listening 6 s.*

**TT16 · "right, right" · working**
Segment 1: "How do I deal with a friend who is always late?"
Segment 2 (play at +2.5 s): *listen 2 s* → "Right, right." *nod* → *keep listening 6 s.*

**TT17 · TV talking in the background · hidden**
Segment 1: "What should I keep in mind before buying a laptop?"
Segment 2 (play at +2 s): *listen silently the whole time.* A TV with people talking is switched on in the room **2 s** into the segment and left on for **4 s**, then switched off. *Stay silent, looking at the screen, 4 s more.*

**TT18 · Hinglish · "hmm... haan" with a long nod · working**
Segment 1: "Mujhe saving shuru karni hai, kaise karun?"
Segment 2 (play at +4 s): *listen 2 s* → "Hmm... haan." *slow nod* → *keep listening 6 s.*

## Group D — Real interruptions (slow stops)

**TT19 · English · "wait wait" · working**
Segment 1: "Tell me about the best places to visit in Rajasthan."
Segment 2 (play at +3 s): *listen 2 s, then lean in slightly* → "Wait wait, sorry, I meant only Jaipur." → *look at the screen, 3 s.*

**TT20 · Hinglish · "nahi nahi" · hidden**
Segment 1: "Mujhe naukri badalni chahiye ya nahi?"
Segment 2 (play at +2.5 s): *listen 2 s* → "Nahi nahi, mera matlab woh nahi tha. Main poochh raha tha ki abhi ya next year?" → *look at the screen, 3 s.*

**TT21 · Hinglish · "ek second" while leaning in · working**
Segment 1: "Mujhe fitness ke liye kuch tips do."
Segment 2 (play at +2 s): *listen 2 s, lean in, raise one hand a little* → "Ek second, ek second... main already gym jaata hoon." → *look at the screen, 3 s.*

**TT22 · English · question in the middle of her answer · working**
Segment 1: "Should I do a master's degree abroad?"
Segment 2 (play at +4 s): *listen 2 s* → "But what about the cost?" → *look at the screen, 3 s.*

**TT23 · English · soft, quiet interruption · hidden**
Segment 1: "Tell me how to handle exam stress."
Segment 2 (play at +3 s): *listen 2 s* → in a quiet voice: "Actually, can we change the topic?" → *look at the screen, 3 s.*

**TT24 · Starts like a "haan", turns into an interruption · working**
Segment 1: "How can I sleep better at night?"
Segment 2 (play at +2.5 s): *listen 2 s* → "Haan, but... I already tried all of that." → *look at the screen, 3 s.*

## Group C — Clear finishes (awkward silences)

**TT25 · English · direct question · working**
Segment 1: "What do you think I should do?" *falling tone, then look straight at the screen, mouth closed, stay silent 5 s.*

**TT26 · Hinglish · direct question · working**
Segment 1: "Tumhe kya lagta hai, mujhe jaana chahiye?" *look at the screen, silent 5 s.*

**TT27 · English · short reply to a greeting · hidden**
Segment 1: "Yeah, I'm good. Just a long day." *small smile, look at the screen, silent 5 s.*

**TT28 · English · clearly finished statement · working**
Segment 1: "That's all I wanted to say about it." *falling tone, look at the screen, silent 5 s.*

**TT29 · English · rising-tone question · working**
Segment 1: "You know what I mean?" *rising tone, look at the screen, silent 5 s.*

**TT30 · Hindi · short close with a nod · hidden**
Segment 1: "Bas, itna hi tha." *one nod, look at the screen, silent 5 s.*

## 5. Labels for scoring

When the file lists are built, each script gets these labels (filled from this document, then checked by the founder against the actual take):

| Label | Example |
|---|---|
| Script ID, filmer, split | TT07, F1, working |
| Group | A |
| Exact transcript of each segment, as actually said | "First I'll finish my degree... then maybe a job... or maybe not." |
| Where each pause starts and ends (seconds into the segment) | 4.1–7.2, 9.5–11.4 |
| Where the end of the turn is | 13.0 |
| For B and D: when segment 2 is played, and where the sound or interruption starts in it | +2.5 s; sound at 2.0 s |
| Noise present | ceiling fan |

The pause and sound times are measured from the actual recording, not copied from this document.
